How accurate is the prediction?

Our platform uses advanced machine learning models trained on extensive datasets to provide a highly accurate risk assessment. However, it's a predictive tool, not a diagnosis.

What data is required for prediction?

We primarily use fingerprint features. You may also be asked for basic health indicators like age, gender, and BMI to enhance accuracy.

{# FAQ Item 3 #}

Is my data secure and private?

Yes, we prioritize your privacy and data security. All uploaded data is encrypted and handled according to strict privacy protocols.

Can I use this for diagnosis?

No, this tool is for risk assessment only and should not replace professional medical advice or diagnosis from a healthcare provider.

A high-risk result indicates a need for further medical consultation. We recommend discussing the results with your doctor.
